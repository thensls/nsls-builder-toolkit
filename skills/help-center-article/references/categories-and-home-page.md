# Categories and the home page

## Categories (exact names)

Pick one. The name must match exactly, including commas and the ampersand.

| Category | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Joining the NSLS | Invitations, nomination codes, cost, deadlines, no chapter at my school |
| Membership, benefits & programs | What membership includes, scholarships, Advanced & Executive programs, the Credit-Recommended Pathway |
| Account & login | Signing in, passwords, Member ID, transferring chapters, chapter info |
| Orders & shipping | Kits, regalia, plaques, shop orders, delivery problems |
| Payments, receipts & charges | Receipts, charges, refunds, payment problems |
| Society by the NSLS | Everything about Society (pick a subcategory) |
| About the NSLS | What the NSLS is, accreditation, legitimacy, privacy, history |
| Induction steps | Orientation, LTD, Speaker Broadcasts, SNT, timelines, credit for events, letters of recommendation |
| Events | National events, summits, attending and getting credit |
| Bring the NSLS to your campus | Starting a chapter, for students and administrators |

**Society by the NSLS subcategories:** Overview; Member Benefits; Institution Benefits; Features - Available Now; Features - Coming Soon; Getting Started; Transition; AI Coaching; Troubleshooting.

If an article doesn't fit any category, don't force it and don't create one. Ask RevOps (message below). New categories need code changes on the home page, so only RevOps adds them.

## The home page doors

The home page shows three tabs: **Thinking about joining**, **Already a member**, **I'm an advisor**. Each tab has cards that link to specific articles. These cards are set in the Help Center code, not in HubSpot's article settings, so publishing an article does **not** put it on the home page.

Ask RevOps to add a door card when the article answers one of the most common questions for that audience. Ask them to remove or change a card when you unpublish an article or change its slug, or the card will lead to "page not found".

## Topic cards

The ten cards under "Browse by topic" show each category with a one-line description. Their order and descriptions are also set in code. Leave the "home page category articles" setting in HubSpot empty: the design shows no article bullets on the cards.
