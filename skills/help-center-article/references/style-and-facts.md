# Voice, terms and facts

Built from the July 2026 Help Center review and style glossary. Anything marked **confirm** has not been locked: ask the MEX lead before putting it in an article, and if they confirm a value, tell RevOps so this file gets updated.

## Voice

- **Talk to the member.** Second person, "you". Use "we" and "our" only when the NSLS explains itself ("We built Society to...").
- **Answer first.** The first sentence answers the title question. Background comes after, if at all.
- **What's in it for them.** Lead with what the member gets or needs to do, not with NSLS process.
- **Assume success.** "When you complete Orientation, you'll unlock..." rather than "If you are able to complete..."
- **Active voice, short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Cut "please note that", "in order to", "it is important to".
- **Warm, confident, never defensive.** For refunds, charges and delays, acknowledge the frustration once and move to the fix.
- **Plain words.** "Members Area", not "portal"; "sign in", not "authenticate".
- **"The NSLS"** on second and later references. "NSLS" alone only as an adjective ("NSLS Shop", "NSLS membership").
- **No superlatives** that need proof ("the only accredited...", "the best...") unless Legal has signed off.
- **No em dashes or en dashes.** See `design-rules.md`.

## Sensitive topics

- **Money (charges, refunds, payment plans):** state the policy plainly, link the self-service page, and give the contact block. Don't promise exceptions.
- **Legitimacy and scams:** factual, not defensive. Name Cognia. No comparisons to other honor societies by name.
- **Deadlines and eligibility:** point to the member's chapter or the Member Experience team for their specific case; don't state chapter-specific dates.
- **Never** include a member's name, email, order number or screenshot with their data.
