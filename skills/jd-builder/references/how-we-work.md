# How We Work: standard copy for every NSLS JD

Status: **APPROVED by Jenna 2026-10-08.** Built from the 5 NSLS core values
(scorecard-builder `founder-sop-framework.md`). The renderer reads every line that starts
with "- " below, in order. Edit the wording here, not inside individual JDs.

- Mission Driven: we test every decision against one question. Are we helping the greatest number of people in the most meaningful way?
- Accountability: we own our commitments and results, and we are honest with each other even when it's hard.
- Get It Done, Make It Fun: we deliver results while building a positive, collaborative team.
- Lead with Heart & Service: we lead with empathy, authenticity and humility.
- Continuous Improvement & Innovation: we choose progress over perfection, including using AI tools to work smarter.
- Remote-first: our team works remotely across the U.S. and collaborates every day through Slack and Google Workspace.
